Black Peak 100 stores the annual summit counts you enter, an optional team name,
and the year of the latest undoable summit in Garmin application storage on your
watch. It reads the watch's local calendar year to assign new entries.

The app does not send those records to the developer, Garmin Connect, a phone,
other watches or any server. It has no network requests, analytics, advertising,
accounts, GPS access or requested permissions. It does not automatically record
activities or detect summits. The Connect IQ Store and Garmin's own services
operate under Garmin's separate privacy policies.

You can correct yearly counts and edit or remove your team name in the app.
There is no cloud backup or cross-watch synchronization. Removing the app or
resetting the watch can remove its local records. Store updates use the same app
identity and storage key to preserve existing records, including supported older
data formats.

For questions, use **Contact Developer** on the application's Connect IQ Store page.
